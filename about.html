About us

About Advikasha

ADVIKASHA GLOBAL FOODS LLP supplies Indian food products and ingredients to traders, retailers, manufacturers, brands and importers. Advikasha is our brand.

Who we are

Recipes are inherited. So is the way we make them.

We are a food business based in Faridabad, Haryana, in India's National Capital Region. Our range covers dry fruits and nuts, makhana, sattu and roasted chana, isabgol (psyllium), jaggery and date sweeteners, whole spices, spice powders and masala, dehydrated vegetables, dried herbs and flowers, seeds and grains, and, for private label, stuffed dates and vitamin and botanical gummies. Buyers come to us for one of three things: wholesale supply by grade, products packed under their own label, or a specific item sourced to their own specification.

How we work

We start with your requirement. Before quoting, we confirm what can be supplied against the grade and parameters you need, and we say plainly when a detail is available only on request. Every quotation states the price basis, packaging, minimum quantity, lead time, payment terms and validity. Samples are arranged on request, and documents are confirmed for each order before dispatch.

Vision

To be a dependable source of Indian food products for businesses that value clear specifications and straight dealing.

Mission

To make sourcing from India simpler for our buyers through accurate product information, responsive communication and written commercial terms.

Leadership

Advikasha is led by Saumya Srivastava, CEO.
