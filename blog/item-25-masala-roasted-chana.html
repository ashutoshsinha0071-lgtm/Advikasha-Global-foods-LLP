    Masala roasted chana: a ready-to-eat snack for retail

    Masala roasted chana is a familiar tea-time snack and an easy addition to any namkeen shelf.

    Advikasha · 21 November 2026 · Item 25 in our catalogue

    About Masala Roasted Chana

    Roasted Bengal gram is coated with spices, salt and edible oil. It is packed fresh to order, and allergen and nutrition details are shared with the sample.

    Grades and specifications buyers ask about

    • Ingredients: roasted Bengal gram, spices, salt, edible oil
    • Even masala coating
    • Crunchy texture
    • Packed fresh to order

    How buyers use it

    • Retail snack packs
    • Namkeen shops
    • Private label
    • Office and café snacks

    Buying sattu and roasted chana

    Roasted gram products are judged on roast, texture and freshness. Sattu should be finely ground and free from grit; roasted chana should be evenly roasted and crunchy. Flavoured chana is packed fresh to order, and allergen and nutrition details are shared with the sample.

    Packing, storage and minimum order

    For domestic trade we pack Masala Roasted Chana in food-grade bags of 5, 10, 20 or 25 kg, with a minimum of 5 kg per item. Larger lots, cartons, retail packs and private-label packing are agreed in the quotation. Keep sealed and cool.

    What to include in your enquiry

    • Pack size
    • Quantity
    • Spice level discussion
    • Destination
    • Delivery city, or country and Incoterm for export

    The quickest way is to send the item number and quantity on WhatsApp, for example “Item 25 – 25 kg”. Prices depend on the quantity of each item; GST and delivery are extra for domestic orders. Samples can be arranged, with sample and courier charges quoted separately.

    Frequently asked questions

    What grades of Masala Roasted Chana can I buy?

    Buyers usually specify: ingredients: roasted Bengal gram, spices, salt, edible oil; even masala coating; crunchy texture; packed fresh to order. The exact grade and parameters are confirmed in the quotation.

    How is Masala Roasted Chana used by businesses?

    Common uses include retail snack packs; namkeen shops; private label; office and café snacks.

    What is the minimum order for Masala Roasted Chana?

    For domestic trade, 5 kg per item, packed in food-grade bags of 5, 10, 20 or 25 kg. Export and private-label minimums are confirmed per order.

    How should Masala Roasted Chana be stored?

    Keep sealed and cool.

    What should I send to get a quote for Masala Roasted Chana?

    Tell us: pack size; quantity; spice level discussion; destination; and your delivery city, or country and Incoterm for export. Or simply send “Item 25 – quantity” on WhatsApp +91 72919 40154.

    Can I get a sample of Masala Roasted Chana?

    Yes, samples can be arranged on request. Sample and courier charges are quoted separately and depend on your destination.

    Do you export Masala Roasted Chana?

    We welcome export enquiries. Specification, documents and Incoterm are agreed in writing, and destination rules on residues, labelling and plant health apply.

    Is sattu seasonal?

    Demand peaks in summer, so planning orders ahead helps secure supply.

    Can I get samples?

    Yes. Sample and courier charges are quoted separately.
