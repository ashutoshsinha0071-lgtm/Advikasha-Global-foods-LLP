About Tomato Roasted Chana

Roasted Bengal gram is coated in tomato seasoning, salt and edible oil, and packed fresh to order.

Grades and specifications buyers ask about

  • Ingredients: roasted Bengal gram, tomato seasoning, salt, edible oil
  • Even red coating
  • Crunchy texture
  • Packed fresh to order

How buyers use it

  • Retail snacks
  • Kids' snack ranges
  • Private label
  • Canteens

Buying sattu and roasted chana

Roasted gram products are judged on roast, texture and freshness. Sattu should be finely ground and free from grit; roasted chana should be evenly roasted and crunchy. Flavoured chana is packed fresh to order, and allergen and nutrition details are shared with the sample.

Packing, storage and minimum order

For domestic trade we pack Tomato Roasted Chana in food-grade bags of 5, 10, 20 or 25 kg, with a minimum of 5 kg per item. Larger lots, cartons, retail packs and private-label packing are agreed in the quotation. Keep sealed and cool.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Pack size
  • Quantity
  • Label needs
  • Destination
  • Delivery city, or country and Incoterm for export

The quickest way is to send the item number and quantity on WhatsApp, for example “Item 27 – 25 kg”. Prices depend on the quantity of each item; GST and delivery are extra for domestic orders. Samples can be arranged, with sample and courier charges quoted separately.

Frequently asked questions

Is sattu seasonal?

Demand peaks in summer, so planning orders ahead helps secure supply.

Can I get samples?

Yes. Sample and courier charges are quoted separately.
