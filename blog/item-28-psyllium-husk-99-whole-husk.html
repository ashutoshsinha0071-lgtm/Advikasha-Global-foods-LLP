Psyllium husk 99%: what the purity grade means

India grows and processes most of the world's psyllium, and husk is traded by purity grade. Understanding the grade helps buyers compare offers fairly.

Advikasha · 21 November 2026 · Item 28 in our catalogue

About Psyllium Husk 99% (Whole Husk)

Psyllium husk is the thin outer coating of the psyllium (isabgol) seed, separated by milling and sieving. Purity, written as a percentage such as 99%, refers to how much of the material is husk rather than other seed parts or light extraneous matter. Higher purity husk is lighter in colour and cleaner.

Grades and specifications buyers ask about

  • Purity: 99% grade
  • Colour: white to off-white
  • Swell volume, if you test for it
  • Moisture and microbiological limits as agreed

How buyers use it

  • Food and supplement manufacturers as an ingredient
  • Retail repacking
  • Bakery and gluten-free baking as a binding ingredient
  • Export to processors

Buying psyllium for manufacturing and trade

Psyllium is traded by purity grade and, for powder, by mesh size. Manufacturers usually test swell volume, moisture and microbiological limits, so share your specification sheet with your enquiry. Label claims for finished products are the brand owner's responsibility and must meet the rules of the market where they are sold.

Packing, storage and minimum order

For domestic trade we pack Psyllium Husk 99% (Whole Husk) in food-grade bags of 5, 10, 20 or 25 kg, with a minimum of 5 kg per item. Larger lots, cartons, retail packs and private-label packing are agreed in the quotation. Store dry and sealed; husk takes up moisture readily.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Purity grade
  • Test parameters you need
  • Quantity
  • Packing and destination
  • Delivery city, or country and Incoterm for export

The quickest way is to send the item number and quantity on WhatsApp, for example “Item 28 – 25 kg”. Prices depend on the quantity of each item; GST and delivery are extra for domestic orders. Samples can be arranged, with sample and courier charges quoted separately.
