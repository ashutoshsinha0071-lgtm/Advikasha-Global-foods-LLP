Walnut kernels for trade: halves, pieces and colour grades

Walnut kernels sell to retailers, bakeries and ice-cream makers, and each of them needs something slightly different. Halves look best in a retail pack, while pieces are the practical choice for baking.

Advikasha · 21 November 2026 · Item 3 in our catalogue

About Walnut Kernels

Walnut kernels are the edible part of the walnut once the shell is removed. They are graded by size (halves, quarters and pieces of different sizes) and by colour, from extra light to amber. Lighter kernels usually command higher prices because they look better and have a milder taste.

Grades and specifications buyers ask about

  • Form: halves, quarters or pieces
  • Colour: extra light, light, light amber or amber
  • Freedom from shell fragments and foreign matter
  • Moisture and freshness as agreed

How buyers use it

  • Retail packs where halves are preferred
  • Cakes, brownies and cookies
  • Ice cream and dessert toppings
  • Chocolate and nut mixes

Buying dry fruits for trade

Dry fruit prices move with harvests, currency and festival demand, so quotations are usually valid for a short period. Ask for the grade, the crop or stock age where relevant, and the packing before comparing prices. For retail and gifting, appearance matters as much as taste; for processing, a lower grade with more broken pieces can be the better value.

Packing, storage and minimum order

For domestic trade we pack Walnut Kernels in food-grade bags of 5, 10, 20 or 25 kg, with a minimum of 5 kg per item. Larger lots, cartons, retail packs and private-label packing are agreed in the quotation. Walnuts contain natural oils and can turn stale in heat; store cool and dry, and use cold storage for long holding.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Form and size
  • Colour grade
  • Quantity
  • Packing and storage needs on arrival
  • Delivery city, or country and Incoterm for export

The quickest way is to send the item number and quantity on WhatsApp, for example “Item 3 – 25 kg”. Prices depend on the quantity of each item; GST and delivery are extra for domestic orders. Samples can be arranged, with sample and courier charges quoted separately.

Frequently asked questions

Can I mix several dry fruits in one order?

Yes. For domestic trade the minimum is 5 kg per item, so you can build one order across several dry fruits.

Do you supply retail packs?

Retail and private-label packing can be discussed. Pack size, material and printing are quoted separately.
