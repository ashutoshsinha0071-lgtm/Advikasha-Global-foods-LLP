Cloves (laung): how buyers judge quality

Cloves are small but expensive, so buyers check them carefully.

Advikasha · 21 November 2026 · Item 40 in our catalogue

About Cloves (Laung)

Cloves are the dried flower buds of the clove tree. Good cloves have intact heads, a reddish-brown colour and a strong aroma. Headless cloves and stems are usually sold as lower grades.

Grades and specifications buyers ask about

  • Intact heads
  • Reddish-brown colour
  • Few stems or headless pieces
  • Moisture as agreed

How buyers use it

  • Retail packs
  • Garam masala and blends
  • Cooking and pickles
  • Food processing

Buying whole spices for trade

Whole spices keep their aroma longer than powders, which is why many buyers grind close to use. Cleaning level, moisture and appearance make the biggest difference to price. For export, residue and contaminant limits depend on the destination market, so share your market's requirements early.

Packing, storage and minimum order

For domestic trade we pack Cloves (Laung) in food-grade bags of 5, 10, 20 or 25 kg, with a minimum of 5 kg per item. Larger lots, cartons, retail packs and private-label packing are agreed in the quotation. Store sealed in a cool, dry place.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Grade
  • Quantity
  • Packing
  • Destination
  • Delivery city, or country and Incoterm for export

The quickest way is to send the item number and quantity on WhatsApp, for example “Item 40 – 25 kg”. Prices depend on the quantity of each item; GST and delivery are extra for domestic orders. Samples can be arranged, with sample and courier charges quoted separately.

Frequently asked questions

What grades of Cloves (Laung) can I buy?

Buyers usually specify: intact heads; reddish-brown colour; few stems or headless pieces; moisture as agreed. The exact grade and parameters are confirmed in the quotation.

How is Cloves (Laung) used by businesses?

Common uses include retail packs; garam masala and blends; cooking and pickles; food processing.

What is the minimum order for Cloves (Laung)?

For domestic trade, 5 kg per item, packed in food-grade bags of 5, 10, 20 or 25 kg. Export and private-label minimums are confirmed per order.

How should Cloves (Laung) be stored?

Store sealed in a cool, dry place.

What should I send to get a quote for Cloves (Laung)?

Tell us: grade; quantity; packing; destination; and your delivery city, or country and Incoterm for export. Or simply send “Item 40 – quantity” on WhatsApp +91 72919 40154.

Can I get a sample of Cloves (Laung)?

Yes, samples can be arranged on request. Sample and courier charges are quoted separately and depend on your destination.

Do you export Cloves (Laung)?

We welcome export enquiries. Specification, documents and Incoterm are agreed in writing, and destination rules on residues, labelling and plant health apply.

What does machine cleaned or sortex mean?

It means the spice has passed through cleaning or colour-sorting machines to remove foreign matter and off-colour seeds.

Can I order several spices together?

Yes. For domestic trade the minimum is 5 kg per item.
