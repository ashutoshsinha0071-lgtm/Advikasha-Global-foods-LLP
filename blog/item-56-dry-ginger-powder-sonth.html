Dry ginger powder (sonth): uses and specifications

Sonth powder is used in tea masala, sweets and spice blends.

Advikasha · 21 November 2026 · Item 56 in our catalogue

About Dry Ginger Powder (Sonth)

Dry ginger powder is made by grinding dried ginger pieces. It is pale beige with a warm, sharp aroma.

Grades and specifications buyers ask about

  • Fineness
  • Pale beige colour
  • Aroma
  • Moisture as agreed

How buyers use it

  • Tea masala
  • Sweets and laddoos
  • Spice blends
  • Bakery

Buying spice powders for food business

Spice powders lose aroma after grinding, so packing and stock age matter. Buyers commonly specify fineness, colour and moisture, and for some products test values such as colour or pungency. Tell us the tests you rely on so the quotation reflects them.

Packing, storage and minimum order

For domestic trade we pack Dry Ginger Powder (Sonth) in food-grade bags of 5, 10, 20 or 25 kg, with a minimum of 5 kg per item. Larger lots, cartons, retail packs and private-label packing are agreed in the quotation. Store sealed and dry.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Fineness
  • Quantity
  • Packing
  • Destination
  • Delivery city, or country and Incoterm for export

The quickest way is to send the item number and quantity on WhatsApp, for example “Item 56 – 25 kg”. Prices depend on the quantity of each item; GST and delivery are extra for domestic orders. Samples can be arranged, with sample and courier charges quoted separately.

Frequently asked questions

What grades of Dry Ginger Powder (Sonth) can I buy?

Buyers usually specify: fineness; pale beige colour; aroma; moisture as agreed. The exact grade and parameters are confirmed in the quotation.

How is Dry Ginger Powder (Sonth) used by businesses?

Common uses include tea masala; sweets and laddoos; spice blends; bakery.

What is the minimum order for Dry Ginger Powder (Sonth)?

For domestic trade, 5 kg per item, packed in food-grade bags of 5, 10, 20 or 25 kg. Export and private-label minimums are confirmed per order.

How should Dry Ginger Powder (Sonth) be stored?

Store sealed and dry.
