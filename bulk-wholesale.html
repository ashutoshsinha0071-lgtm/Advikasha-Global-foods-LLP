Formats

Packing and minimums

Domestic trade orders are packed in food-grade bags of 5, 10, 20 or 25 kg, with a minimum order of 5 kg per item, so you can build one order across many items. Larger lots, cartons and palletisation are agreed in the quotation. Prices depend on the quantity of each item; GST and delivery are extra.

Recurring supply

If you buy the same items every month or season, tell us the expected volume and frequency. Recurring requirements help us plan sourcing and keep your specification consistent from lot to lot.

Your enquiry

Include in your enquiry

  • Item and grade

    Item number from our catalogue, or product and grade, e.g. Item 2 – Cashew W320.

  • Quantity

    Per item, and whether it is one-time or recurring.

  • Parameters

    Moisture, size grade, purity or any limits you test against, if relevant.

  • Packaging

    Bag size, labelling or custom packing needs.

  • Delivery

    City and state for domestic orders; country and preferred Incoterm for export.
