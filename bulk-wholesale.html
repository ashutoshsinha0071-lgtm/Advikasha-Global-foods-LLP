Bulk supply and wholesale

Wholesale supply for traders, retailers, distributors, food manufacturers and food-service buyers, with packaging and terms agreed in writing.

Request a quotationDownload catalogue (PDF)

Formats

Packing and minimums

Domestic trade orders are packed in food-grade bags of 5, 10, 20 or 25 kg, with a minimum order of 5 kg per item, so you can build one order across many items. Larger lots, cartons and palletisation are agreed in the quotation. Prices depend on the quantity of each item; GST and delivery are extra.

Recurring supply

If you buy the same items every month or season, tell us the expected volume and frequency. Recurring requirements help us plan sourcing and keep your specification consistent from lot to lot.

Your enquiry

Include in your enquiry

  • Item and grade

    Item number from our catalogue, or product and grade, e.g. Item 2 – Cashew W320.

  • Quantity

    Per item, and whether it is one-time or recurring.

  • Parameters

    Moisture, size grade, purity or any limits you test against, if relevant.

  • Packaging

    Bag size, labelling or custom packing needs.

  • Delivery

    City and state for domestic orders; country and preferred Incoterm for export.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What bag sizes do you pack in?

Food-grade bags of 5, 10, 20 or 25 kg for domestic trade. Larger lots, cartons and palletisation are agreed in the quotation.

Can you supply the same items every month?

Yes. Tell us the expected volume and frequency so we can plan sourcing and keep your specification consistent.

How are wholesale prices worked out?

Prices depend on the item, grade and quantity. GST and delivery are extra for domestic orders.

What should I include in a wholesale enquiry?

Item number or product and grade, quantity per item, any test parameters, packaging and your delivery city.
