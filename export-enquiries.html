International buyers

Export enquiries

We welcome enquiries from importers, distributors and brands outside India. Specification, samples, delivery terms and documents are agreed in writing before any order. Importer-Exporter Code (IEC): ACOFA3007F.

Sourcing

Product sourcing

Our range covers dry fruits and nuts, makhana, sattu and roasted chana, isabgol (psyllium), jaggery and date sweeteners, whole spices, spice powders and masala, dehydrated vegetables, dried herbs and flowers, seeds and grains, and, for private label, stuffed dates and vitamin and botanical gummies. Import rules differ by country, for example on pesticide residues, microbiology, labelling and plant health. Please share your market's requirements with your enquiry so they are reflected in the quotation. Export prices and packing are quoted separately from our domestic trade terms.

Process

How an international enquiry works

  1. 01

    Requirement

    Product, grade, parameters, quantity, packaging, destination port or city, and preferred Incoterm.

  2. 02

    Specification check

    We confirm the specification we can supply and list any parameter that needs testing or is available on request.

  3. 03

    Samples

    Pre-shipment samples can be arranged. Sample quantity, charges and courier costs are confirmed for your destination.

  4. 04

    Quotation

    A written quotation with currency, Incoterm and named place, packaging, MOQ, payment terms, lead time and validity.

  5. 05

    Order and documents

    After confirmation, the documents for your shipment are agreed and the goods are packed and dispatched as quoted.

Packaging & documents

Export packaging and documentation

Packaging for international shipments is agreed per order. Typical options include lined bags or sacks and cartons. Labelling in line with your market requirements and palletisation can be discussed.

Documents depend on the product, the destination and the shipment. We confirm availability before you order.

  • Standard

    Commercial invoice and packing list.

  • Confirmed per order

    Certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of analysis, third-party laboratory reports, and MSDS where applicable.

  • Destination-specific

    Any additional document your import authority requires; tell us early so we can check it.

Terms

Incoterms and freight

Prices are quoted on the Incoterm agreed for your order, for example EXW, FCA, FOB or CIF, always with the named place or port. Where freight is included, the quotation states what it covers. If you use your own forwarder, quote on FOB or FCA terms.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you export food products from India?

Yes, we welcome enquiries from importers, distributors and brands. Our Importer-Exporter Code is ACOFA3007F.

Which Incoterms do you quote?

Prices are quoted on the Incoterm agreed for your order, such as EXW, FCA, FOB or CIF, always with the named place or port.

Which export documents can you provide?

A commercial invoice and packing list are standard. Certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of analysis and laboratory reports are confirmed per order.

Can I get a pre-shipment sample?

Yes. Sample quantity, charges and courier costs are confirmed for your destination.

What is the minimum order for export?

Export minimums depend on the product, packing and shipment, and are stated in the quotation.
