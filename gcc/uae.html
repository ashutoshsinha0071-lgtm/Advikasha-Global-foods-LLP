Wholesale supply from India for the UAE

We supply Indian food products and ingredients to importers, wholesale traders, supermarket buyers and private-label brands in Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi and across the Emirates, including buyers who re-export into the wider GCC.

Market

Supplying UAE from India

The UAE is one of the largest markets for Indian spices and a regional hub: many ingredients landed at Jebel Ali are repacked or re-exported to Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait, Qatar and Bahrain.

It is also one of the main destinations for Indian makhana. Government figures put the UAE at about 17% of India's makhana exports, behind the United States and Canada.

Under the India–UAE Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA), in force since May 2022, many Indian food products enter at reduced or zero duty, subject to the agreement's rules of origin. Whether a CEPA certificate of origin applies is confirmed for each product and shipment.

Import requirements

What to share with your enquiry

Retail packs sold in the UAE normally need Arabic labelling in line with UAE and GSO standards. Send us your label requirements early so packing can be planned.

If the goods will be re-exported from the UAE to another GCC country, tell us the final market as well; documents and labelling may need to suit both.

Documents depend on the product and shipment. A commercial invoice and packing list are standard; certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of analysis and third-party laboratory reports are confirmed per order. See quality and documents.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you supply UAE importers from India?

Yes. We quote importers, wholesale traders and brands in the UAE. Tell us the product, grade, quantity, packing and destination port, usually Jebel Ali.

Can you quote CIF Jebel Ali?

We quote on the Incoterm agreed for your order, for example FOB Indian port or CIF Jebel Ali. Where freight is included, the quotation says what it covers.

Do Indian food products get lower duty in the UAE under CEPA?

Many Indian food products enter the UAE at reduced or zero duty under CEPA, subject to its rules of origin. Whether it applies to your product is checked per order.

Can you pack makhana or spices under our brand for the UAE market?

Yes, private-label packing can be discussed. Label content, including Arabic text, is agreed with you before production.
