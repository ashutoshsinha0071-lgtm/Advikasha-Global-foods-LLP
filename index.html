From a WhatsApp message to dispatch

  1. 1

    Send your list

    Item numbers and quantities on WhatsApp, for example “Item 44 – 25 kg”, or through the enquiry form. Add the grade or specification you work to.

  2. 2

    Get a written quotation

    Price by quantity, packing and delivery terms. For domestic orders GST and delivery are shown separately.

  3. 3

    Approve a sample

    If you want one. Sample and courier charges are quoted for your city or country.

  4. 4

    Packing and dispatch

    Packed in food-grade bags of 5, 10, 20 or 25 kg, or as agreed, and dispatched after you confirm the order.

We quote against the grade you need, and say plainly when a detail is only available on request.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does Advikasha supply?

Dry fruits and nuts, makhana, sattu and roasted chana, isabgol (psyllium), jaggery and date sweeteners, whole spices, spice powders, dehydrated vegetables, dried herbs and flowers, and seeds and grains — 83 products in all — plus private-label stuffed dates and gummies.

What is the minimum order?

For domestic trade, 5 kg per item, so you can build one order across many products. Export and private-label minimums are confirmed per order.

Do you supply buyers outside India?

Yes, we welcome export enquiries. Specification, samples, Incoterms and documents are agreed in writing before any order.

Can you pack products under my brand?

Private-label packing can be discussed for most of the range. Pack format, artwork and minimums are confirmed per project.
