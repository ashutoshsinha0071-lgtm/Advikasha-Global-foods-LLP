How to Specify Peanut Butter Choc Crunch for a Wholesale Order

Write a specification for Peanut Butter Choc Crunch: identity, grade, test methods and acceptance checks for wholesale buyers and importers.

Advikasha buyer guides · Stuffed Dates · Published 25 November 2026

  1. Define the material and application
  2. Turn grade words into checkable requirements
  3. Use a specification and evidence table
  4. Approve the version that will be purchased
  5. Frequently asked questions
  6. Discuss your requirement

A useful Peanut Butter Choc Crunch wholesale specification connects the product description to the result your business needs. Start with accepted date pieces, filling performance, component allergens and the finished gift or snack format. This gives the supplier a defined brief and gives the receiving team an acceptance basis when the lot arrives.

Define the material and application

Creamy roasted-peanut filling with whey protein, chopped peanuts and protein crisps, set inside a pitted date.

For this purchase, focus on private-label snack pouches, discovery boxes and gifting built around peanut-butter texture, chopped peanuts and protein-crisp distribution. Keep the description specific enough that an alternative form or grade cannot be supplied under the same informal name.

Turn grade words into checkable requirements

  • peanut-butter texture, chopped peanuts and protein-crisp distribution
  • Physical form and agreed size or piece weight
  • Performance in the intended application
  • Agreed laboratory parameters and methods

For each parameter, record the unit, method, acceptance basis and the evidence that will be supplied. If the parameter is not yet agreed, leave it open for discussion in the enquiry rather than filling it with an assumed standard.

Use a specification and evidence table

RequirementWhat the buyer recordsEvidence to agree
IdentityExact name, form and source where relevantSupplier specification and sample code
Physical gradeThe agreed count, size, cut, mesh or piece basisMeasurement method and accepted reference
ApplicationThe intended production or preparation methodControlled trial record
Lot releaseBuyer-requested parameters and agreed limitsWhich tests and reports are available for the lot

Use a versioned filling brief, piece-weight observations and a final-pack trial. Keep requested testing separate from results that have actually been measured.

Approve the version that will be purchased

Ask the receiving and production teams to review the same document. Save the version number on the quotation and order. A changed ingredient, form, process or pack should be reviewed before it is treated as an equivalent repeat supply.

Frequently asked questions

What should the Peanut Butter Choc Crunch specification start with?

Start with the exact product identity and intended application. Private-label snack pouches, discovery boxes and gifting built around peanut-butter texture, chopped peanuts and protein-crisp distribution.

Which quality evidence should I request?

Request a versioned filling brief, piece-weight observations and a final-pack trial. Agree the methods, limits and lot reference in writing.

What should I send Advikasha for a quotation?

Send the exact product, target specification, quantity, packaging, application and delivery city or country. This is a private-label development concept. Final recipe, piece weights, packing, minimum quantity and lead time are confirmed after the buyer brief and sample review.
