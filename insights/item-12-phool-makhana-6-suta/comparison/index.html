For Phool Makhana – 6 Suta, the most useful comparison is 6 suta versus 5+ suta makhana; agree a sample and sieve basis before comparing the premium. Compare the alternatives against the same finished-product brief. Price, physical appearance and preparation work should be evaluated together.

Keep identity and form explicit

Large, white popped fox nuts. Top grade for gifting and snacks.

Write a separate specification for each alternative. A shared common name, similar colour or similar-looking pack does not establish equivalent composition, grade or application performance.

Select the grade using the final cooked, roasted or milled application. Record how many pieces survive the handling sequence and how the finished product fills the chosen pack. Trade size names should be backed by an accepted sample and a defined measurement basis.

Run a matched sample trial

Measure broken pieces before and after a pilot roast and record the filled volume of a weighed portion.

Give samples neutral codes during the comparison where practical. Agree the observations first and keep the same input basis. If one alternative needs an additional preparation step, record its time and loss instead of hiding them inside the final sensory score.

Choose by usable value

Use the alternative that meets the application and commercial brief. Size premium, breakage and volumetric freight; a light product can fill a large carton.

Keep both specifications on file if the business uses different forms for different products. A substitute should have its own approval rather than being introduced as an undocumented replacement.
