Privacy policy

ADVIKASHA GLOBAL FOODS LLP

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how ADVIKASHA GLOBAL FOODS LLP ("Advikasha", "we") handles personal data submitted through this website. We process personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Details you enter in our quotation and contact forms: name, company, email, phone or WhatsApp number, country, delivery details, product requirements and any message.
  • Basic technical data needed to run and protect the site.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations and samples, and carry out orders you place with us.
  • To keep a record of business communication.
  • To protect the website against spam and misuse.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your enquiry details for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that host this website and deliver our email. When your order requires it, we share relevant details with the logistics partners and authorities involved in the shipment.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to respond and to maintain business records, and in any case as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your enquiry data where we are not required to keep it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent you have given, by writing to ashutosh@advikasha.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Enquiry list in your browser

When you add products to your enquiry list, only the product names are stored in your own browser so the list stays while you browse. Your name, email, phone number and message are not stored there. Use “Clear list” to remove it. The enquiry form is delivered to us through our website host; if you choose “Send on WhatsApp instead”, the message is sent by WhatsApp under its own policy.

Security

No system is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive personal documents through the website.
