Makhana is the popped seed of the fox nut plant (Euryale ferox), grown mainly in Bihar. Phool makhana is traded by suta, a size grade: the higher the suta number, the larger the popped nut. Larger grades suit gifting and premium snacks; regular sizes suit cooking and roasting.

Grades and formats

  • Phool makhana 6 suta: large, white popped nuts for gifting and premium snacks.
  • Phool makhana 5+ suta: medium-large, a popular grade for roasting.
  • Phool makhana 4 suta: regular size for cooking, kheer and vrat use.
  • Roasted makhana: pink salt & black pepper, peri peri, cheese & herbs, pudina, tangy tomato and sour cream & onion. Packed fresh to order.

Private label

Roasted makhana can be packed under your brand. Share the flavours, pack size and target quantity, and we will confirm the minimum order, packaging and lead time in the quotation.

What to send with your enquiry

The grade or flavour, quantity, pack type (bulk or retail) and delivery destination.
