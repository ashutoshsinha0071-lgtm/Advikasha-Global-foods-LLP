Images are representative. Product descriptions refer to typical uses of these ingredients and are not health claims.

Jaggery (gur) is an unrefined sweetener made by boiling down cane juice. It is sold in blocks for traditional use and as a free-flowing powder that is easier to dose in recipes. Palm jaggery is made from palm sap, and date powder is ground from dried dates.

What we supply

  • Jaggery (gur) block: sugarcane jaggery in block form.
  • Jaggery powder: free-flowing brown sugarcane jaggery powder.
  • Palm jaggery powder.
  • Date powder: for drinks, bakery and mixes.

Who buys them

Sweet makers, bakeries, beverage and health-food brands, grocery wholesalers and importers.

What to send with your enquiry

The product, quantity, pack size and destination. Colour and moisture expectations can be agreed in the quotation.
