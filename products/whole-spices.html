Images are representative. Product descriptions refer to typical uses of these ingredients and are not health claims.

Whole spices keep their aroma longer than ground spices and are the starting point for most masala and seasoning blends. Each item is quoted with its grade or quality name, so two quotations can be compared on the same basis.

What we supply

  • Pepper and cardamom: black pepper, white pepper (grade A), green cardamom 6–7 mm (Kerala origin) and black cardamom.
  • Bark, buds and leaves: cloves, cinnamon sticks or bark pieces, mace blades and bay leaves.
  • Seed spices: cumin (Singapore 99% quality), coriander, fennel (sortex cleaned), bold ajwain and fenugreek seeds.
  • Chilli and ginger: whole dry red chilli (A grade) and dry ginger (sonth), without sulphur.

Who buys them

Spice traders, masala manufacturers, restaurants and caterers, repackers and importers in the GCC, UK, Europe and the Americas.

What to send with your enquiry

Item number and grade, quantity per item, packing and destination, plus any cleaning standard or lab tests your buyers expect.
