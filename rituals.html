Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which herbs and flowers are in the Rituals range?

Tulsi, hibiscus (roselle), lemongrass, chamomile, rose petals and butterfly pea flowers.

Can you create a custom herbal tea blend?

Custom blends can be discussed for brands, with samples for approval before production.

Can you supply tea-bag cut?

Cut sizes for loose tea and tea bags can be discussed. Tell us the size range you use.

Do you make health claims for these herbs?

No. Descriptions refer to typical culinary and tea uses and are not health claims.
